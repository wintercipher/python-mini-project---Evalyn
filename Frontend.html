Answer Sheet Evaluator

Upload a typed answer key and a student’s answer sheet (PDF) with selectable text to get question-wise marks, a total and a Pass/Fail result. Scanned and handwritten pages aren't supported.

Answer key format

One block per question. Types: mcq, tf, blank, numeric, short, long. Student answers should start with 1., Q1 or similar.

Both PDFs must contain selectable typed text. Scanned and handwritten pages aren't supported.

Answer: B

Keywords: chlorophyll, sunlight, glucose
